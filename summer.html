Cross the swollen river on the boat bridge and walk north through the flooded rice terraces, the lantern village and the bamboo, over the ford and up the hydrangea steps to the temple. Fighters wait on the road, and they are different men every journey; two hard duels guard the climb, and each side path keeps a fighter of its own. Ambushers rise out of the reeds and the straw. Your wounds stay with you, and if you fall the journey is over. Beside the road: a teahouse on stilts, two inns, two dojos, three shrines, and a masked stranger with dice. The rain comes and goes.
