Walk the spring road north from the south gate, through the tea fields, over the red bridge, into the bamboo, up the climb and through the thousand torii to the shrine where the Petal Blade will wait. Six fighters wait on the way: walk up to one and the duel begins where you stand. Each one you beat pays you, your wounds stay with you, and if you fall the journey is over. Beside the road: an inn and a teahouse to rest and buy, two dojos to learn techniques, two shrines for blessings, a hot spring, and a dice player.
