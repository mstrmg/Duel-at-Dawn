# Duel at Dawn — Project Context

## The game
A 3D third-person samurai dueling roguelike in an ink wash style, drawn like pen and ink, built in Three.js. The camera sits behind the player's shoulder and stays framed on the opponent ahead. A lone swordsman travels a branching road across 4 seasonal regions to face the Sword Saint in a final duel at dawn. Each run is one journey; death ends the run.

## Color (Miles 2026-11-05: the game is about color)
The journey starts in **Winter**, then Spring, then Summer (the rest of the order is not decided). The world is ink; color is earned. The wanderer wins back a quarter of his color in each region: he leaves Winter with his **coat** in color (indigo, `HERO_COLORS.coat` in models.js), so he enters Spring at 1/4, and beating the Spring boss colors his **hakama** (the petals' rose) so he reaches Summer at 1/2. `run.colors` holds which parts are colored; `FighterModel.setColors()` paints them. Each region's boss should make color central to its fight.

## Core combat (decided, built in the combat prototype)
- Short, deadly duels: most fighters die in 1–3 hits. The player has 3 vitality, enemies 2; a fighter caught staggered, winded or after a whiff takes +1 damage.
- **Two stances, High and Low**, set with Q (High) and E (Low). Not three stances, and not chosen with the mouse. Your guard covers your stance's line; a strike into the line he is not guarding winds up faster. Guard (hold right click or K) only stops a blow in the line you are guarding.
- **Perfect parry**: raise a matching guard just as the blow lands; it staggers the attacker and opens a killing counter.
- **One strike button; footwork picks the cut.** No extra attack buttons and no randomness:
  - Strike with W held: lunging thrust. Long reach, narrow (a sidestep beats it), slow to recover.
  - Strike with S held: retreating cut. Short, gives ground, safe if it misses.
  - Strike with A or D held: side cut. Wide, keeps circling, catches a sideways evade.
  - No direction held: the ordinary cut.
- **Footwork**: A/D circle the opponent at the same distance, W/S close or open it. Space evades (with i-frames); Space with W is a quicker step in without i-frames. Dashed reach arcs on the ground. Fighters visibly step: feet lift and hakama legs swing with the actual motion, never sliding.
- **Feints**: raise guard during your own windup to pull the strike back and bait his guard.
- **Answering a block** (Miles 2026-11-05): a strike within 0.9 s of blocking deals 1 wound only (never the +1 for catching him open), lunges after him across the shove, and is safe if it misses (`ANSWER` in config.js).
- **Guard out of your own cut** (Miles 2026-11-05, "don't change the AI"): the player only (`quickGuard` on PLAYER_DEF) can raise guard straight out of strike/recover once the cut is thrown, to block or parry an evade-and-punish (`QUICK_GUARD`).
- **Breath = stamina.** Attacking, blocking, dodging, feinting and switching stance cost breath; standing still refills it. Out of breath = winded and vulnerable.
- **Draw-cut (iai)**: hold to sheathe and charge, release to strike. A full charge is unblockable and lethal; missing leaves you exposed.
- **Enemies have readable tells**: the blade drifts to the new line, then a windup with a red glint.
- **Enemies punish mashing and lunge spam**: they answer every strike, parry more under sustained pressure, strike back after a block or evade (a blocked lunge always gets punished), and guard out of their own windup when beaten to the punch. Careful play that reads tells and punishes openings wins; mashing does not.
- Enemies so far: the **Ronin** (close range, mirrors your line, feints, double strikes) and the **Spearman** (keeps you at range: stop-thrusts walk-ins, sweeps circlers, meets lunges with the spearhead, shoves you off up close).

## Roguelike systems (planned)
- Map of stops: duel, elite duel, inn (heal/shop), shrine (blessing), dojo (learn techniques), event, boss.
- Wounds instead of HP (built): every cut is a named wound by where it landed (brow, thigh, side, forearm) that takes something away (tells hidden, no evade, guard slips, no draw-cut), for the player and for enemies; they carry between fights and are mended at inns, the teahouse or the hot spring. `src/wounds.js`.
- Arts, not stat boosts (Miles 2026-11-05: abilities must change the game past stats): techniques, blessings and charms each add a rule. 21 so far: techniques Cutting Down, Answering Blade, Flowing Guard, Second Draw, Slip Inside, Iron Gate, Remaining Mind, Striking Down, Hair's Breadth, Swallow Return; blessings Petal, Wind, Fox, Rice, Pine, Moon; charms Bell, Sageo, Iron tsuba, Blinding powder, White headband. Kept as ids in def.arts, checked with Fighter.has().
- Offers are randomized per journey (Miles 2026-11-05): `rollStock` in stops.js, seeded by `run.seed`, deals techniques to the dojos (3 each), blessings to the shrines (2 each) and charms to the teahouse (2) and inn (1).
- Colored indicators (Miles 2026-11-05): gaining an art, taking a wound or having one mended stamps a colored seal (`src/gain.js`: technique blue 技, blessing pink 祝, charm gold 守, wound red 傷, mended green 癒); the same colors mark them in the run panel and stop cards. Wounded fighters show the cut where it landed: a gash decal with blood soaking out and drips (Miles asked for better than a red band), built in `FighterModel.buildWounds`.
- Build from: techniques (3 equipped), swords with traits, charms (passives), vows (power with restrictions).
- Honor system: bow before duels or strike first/cheat; high and low honor unlock different paths.
- Meta-progression: on death the sword passes to a new wanderer (lineage), gaining a permanent trait; a home dojo unlocks techniques, swords, and characters between runs.

## Art & feel
A pen and ink look: black ink on paper tones, hatched shading, hand-drawn ink outlines that wobble slightly, lots of empty space and mist, red as the main accent color. Each season may add one accent of its own: Spring's is pink (cherry blossoms, azaleas, wildflowers, falling petals), so the vegetation pops against the ink. Rendering is custom (hatch shader plus an edge-detected ink line pass), not realistic lighting. Fighters are detailed figures (kimono, hakama, hats, armor) built from primitives. Each enemy carries one small detail in its own color (Miles: unique, not big; `ACCENTS` in models.js): Ronin indigo kasa band, Wandering Ronin ochre gourd, Spearman green tassel, Bandit persimmon headband, Masked Duelist violet flute bag, Temple Guard gold beads, Deserter teal banner, Yamabushi sky-blue pompoms. The Petal Blade is the exception: all his clothes are pink while he holds the stolen color, and drain to ink as he loses it. Ambushers are plain, no color (Miles). Minimal sound: wind, footsteps, blade rings. Killing blows slow time with a red brush stroke.
